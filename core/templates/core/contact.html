Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password? ➕
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your email and follow the instructions sent to your inbox.
Can I play on multiple devices? ➕
Yes! Your Gaming Hub account works across all devices. Simply log in with your credentials on any device.
How do I report a player? ➕
Click on the player's profile during or after a match and select "Report Player". Our moderation team will review the report within 24 hours.
